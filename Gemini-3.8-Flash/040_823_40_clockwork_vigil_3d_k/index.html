THE GRAND LIFT: Locked while any watch enemy lives (HUD tracks Cull: n/7). When 7/7 culled, a bell rings and the lift unlocks (LIFT READY). Board and press [E] to wind it up. Rises for 8s to the Heart Chamber with two mid-ride ambushes: 2 Drudges climb up at 2s from the left chain, and 1 Bulwark drops down at 5s. Moving lift has open edges: enemies knocked off are destroyed with GRIND; falling off deals 20 damage and returns you to lift base.
THE HEART CHAMBER: High above the lift, holding The Heartwarden (400 HP, 120 overload), 2 chamber piston crushers, 2 arena gear pits, and 2 chamber coolant taps.
MASTER KEY & 3 TUNINGS: Slain Heartwarden drops the Master Key without freezing play. Step onto it to view the Vigil Report and choose 1 tuning: [1] Coolant Lines (70 Running Hot & natural cooling cutoff, 2s overheat), [2] Torque Springs (+50% knockback & burst), [3] Fine Gearing (-3 heat on J and K). Persists across deaths!
VIGIL REPORT (TAB): Hold [Tab] anytime for full mission stats (Total Time, Deaths, GRIND/CRUSH Executions, Overheats, Bursts Used, Boss Kills).
