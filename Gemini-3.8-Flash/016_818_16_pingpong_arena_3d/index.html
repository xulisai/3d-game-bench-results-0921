Official Match Regulations:
• Match Format: Best-of-3 games. Each game to 11 points. At 10–10 (deuce), game continues until a player leads by 2 (11–10 does not win). First to 2 games wins match.
• Deciding Game & Change of Ends: At 1–1, Game 3 is deciding! Players switch ends at the start and when either player first reaches 5 points. Controls naturally adapt to your new side without stamina refill.
• Key Point Replays & Stats: Game/Match points and 8+ hit rallies replay in slow motion from a side-on camera (skip with Enter). Full tournament stats sheet presented after the handshake.
• Service Rotation: Service changes every 2 points; from 10–10 onward, service changes every 1 point. Server alternates game by game.
• Advanced Techniques: Hold J for heavy Loop Drive, tap on the rise for fast Counter-Hit, L for short Drop Shot, I for aggressive wrist Flick over the net.
• Net Cords & Edges: Net clips in rally remain live (NET CORD); on serve they trigger a LET replay. Top edge strikes are IN (EDGE); side face hits are OUT.
• Stamina: Sprints, loops, and sidesteps drain stamina; stamina lowers movement and shrinks the sweet zone! Between-game breaks recover stamina.
• Opponent Spin Reading: Opponent spin labels are hidden! Read the AI's spin directly from their swing silhouette (high brush = topspin, chop = backspin, cross sweep = sidespin) and ball arc.
