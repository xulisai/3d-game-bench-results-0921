Skylark Angler

Golden Hour Skies • Cloud Sea Fishing

Welcome aboard the airship Skylark!

  • Fly above the golden cloud sea using WSAD and QE.
  • Lighthouse Checkpoints: Fly within 5u of Sunwatch, Southreach, or Mistveil Beacon to light it! Crashing pays 20g salvage fee to respawn at active beacon (up to 3 respawns).
  • The Reckoning [K]: Press K at the dock to End the Day, or face the final settlement on Game Over. Day ratings: S (1500g + whale hunt), A (1000g), B (500g), C.
  • The Skylark Cup [J]: 150s contest with rotating targets (×3 score). Auto-sold at finish with trophy bonuses (Bronze 400+, Silver 700+, Gold 1000+).
  • The Whale Hunt [H]: Accept at pier board. Strike surfaced Cloud Whale with harpoon (F) for 90s Sleighride tow! Winch breaches for +20g.
  • Tackle [G] & Depth [1/2/3]: Light/Heavy hooks, 4 species with unique fight scripts, consumable line spools, and cargo buoyancy tiers.
