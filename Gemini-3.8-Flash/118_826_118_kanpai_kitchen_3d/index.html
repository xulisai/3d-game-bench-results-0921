Controls & Kitchen Rules

  • WASD Move  |  C Camera  |  T (Hold) ×12 speed  |  R Reset
  • Two Hand Slots: Carry up to 2 items/dishes (HAND 1 & 2). 3rd fetch gives HANDS FULL. Serving, loading, and chopping act on the first occupied slot.
  • Stove Outages: Burner A goes down during [95, 120) (warning 90–95s); Burner B goes down during [140, 165) (warning 135–140s). Loading is blocked (STOVE DOWN), cooking clocks freeze, and plating is blocked (FROZEN).
  • Nori Line Cook (Key N): Press N to cycle Nori's post: WOK (auto-plates burners at 1.15·T to Pass Shelf), PREP (unattended chopping), WASH (unattended washing). Press E at Pass Shelf to pick up dishes!
  • 2-Slot Cutting Board Queue & Swap: Holds up to 2 raw items, chops sequentially (2.0s each). Hands full with a raw item? Press E to SWAP it for the waiting chopped result!
  • Closed Plate Loop & Doneness: Pool of 4 plates (PLATES n/4). Plating succeeds only when SERVABLE (0.70–1.30·T; 1.00–1.20·T is ★ PERFECT). 20s delayed dirty return on serve.
  • Stations & Recipes:
  • • Burners A & B: MISO SOUP (6s), FRIED RICE (8s), RAMEN (10s), KANPAI STIR-FRY (12s)
    • Oven: YAKITORI (10s), DUMPLINGS (12s)
    • Pass Hatch: Serve plated dish to oldest ticket (+1 SERVED)
    • Sink: Wash plate (4.0s)  |  Holding item? Press E to scrap!
