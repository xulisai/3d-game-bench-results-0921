EMBERWAY CARAVAN
A journey of commerce and survival through the Golden Valley

• Goal: Guide your covered mule wagon to the Capital holding at least 500 crowns.

• Goods & Hold: Trade Salt, Resin, Marsh Silk, Iron, Spice, and Timber across 6 posting stations. Timber is bought only at Pinegate. Wagon hold capacity is 16 units.

• Reputation & Tier 2: Earn +1 reputation by completing station contracts or making single purchases of 40 👑 or more. Reach Reputation 2+ to become a Tier 2 partner with 10% discounts on buy & sell prices!

• Durability & Camping: Wagon starts with 10 durability. Hazards inflict damage (-2). At 0 durability, broken cargo loses 5 👑 daily. Camp on the road once per segment to restore durability to 10 [-1 Bread].

• Ferry Toll: Fordholt ➔ Ironford crossing costs 30 crowns or 3 timber.

• Winter: From Day 20, snow falls, highlands take 2x travel time, and Capital spice sells at 70 👑.

• Contracts & Hirelings: Fenhollow & Ironford offer contracts. Hire Mira, Bran, or Petra (max 2) for 20 👑 each.

• Controls: Press Enter to Start. Press L for Ledger. Press P for Reputation. Press R to restart.
