LONGBOW MARKS
Olympic Recurve Archery Championship — 70 Meters

Rules & Controls

• Aim: Move Mouse or WASD / Arrows to steer your sight pin. Aim sway wanders in a continuous Lissajous rhythm.

• Draw: Hold Space to draw the bow (reaches 100% full draw in 1.20s).

• Clicker: Listen for the metallic click at 100% full draw.

• Release Window: Release Space within ±0.04 s of the clicker for a clean full-draw release!

• Sight Marks: Use keys 1–4 for 30 / 50 / 70 / 90 m sight marks (exact flat-field drop compensation for standard arrows).

• Arrow Types: Choose Light (+8% v0, 1.25× drift), Standard (60 m/s), or Heavy (-8% v0, 0.8× drift) before the match.

• The X-Ring: Hits within r ≤ 0.03 m are recorded as an X (10 pts). Leaderboard ties are broken by X count!

• Breath Bar: Hold Shift to halve sway growth (drains 30/s from 100). Depleting to 0 triggers fatigue penalty (sway ×1.5) until regenerated to ≥50 (regens 25/s).

• Crosswind & Windsock: Observe windsock and HUD readout to compensate against horizontal drift.

• Ranking Round: 12 Ends × 3 Arrows = 36 Arrows. Complete scorecard and compete live against AI-1 (327) and AI-2 (318)!

• Elimination Bracket: Advance to the 4-archer bracket seeded by score: AI-3 (336), AI-1 (327), AI-2 (318), and you! Semifinal: YOU vs AI-2; Final: winner vs AI-3.

• Set System: Sets of 3 arrows (+2 set pts for set win, +1 each for tie). First to 6 set points wins! AI set totals: AI-2 shoots 26 ([9,9,8]), AI-3 shoots 28 ([10,9,9]).

• Field Course: 3 Targets (30m Uphill at +10°, 50m Flat, 90m Downhill at -5°), 3 arrows each (9 arrows total).
