Cadence Blade

Rhythm-Combat Arena — Neon Duelling Amphitheatre
Tempo Zones: Zone A (120 BPM) & Zone B (150 BPM)
The arena is split by a luminous glowing floor seam into two halves:
• ZONE A (Starting Half, Z ≥ 0): 120 BPM (0.50s beat / 2.0s bar).
• ZONE B (Far Half, Z < 0): 150 BPM (0.40s beat / 1.6s bar).
• Crossing the seam shifts the song tempo at the next bar boundary accompanied by a one-bar transition drum fill. Enemies automatically adapt their strike grids to the player's active zone!
The 8-Bar Phrase & Combat Dynamics
Music runs in an 8-bar phrase (16s) with an ending drum fill in Bar 8.
• Bars 1–4 (OPEN half): Your attack window! Beat Puppets strike only on Bar 2 Beat 1; Syncopation Puppets strike only on Bar 3 Beats 2 & 4.
• Bars 5–8 (PRESSURE half): Dense enemy assault! Beat Puppets strike Bars 5 & 7 (Beat 1); Syncopation Puppets strike Bars 5, 6 & 7 (Beats 2 & 4).
Phrase Burst & Parries
Landing 8 consecutive ON BEAT hits (attacks only) arms the PHRASE BURST: the fencer glows with golden energy, and the next attack within 2 beats deals ×3 damage! If the window lapses or an attack misses, the charge dissipates.
Hold SHIFT/K to guard (×0.5 DMG); press guard within ±0.08s of any beat for a PERFECT PARRY. 3 consecutive off-beats trigger RHYTHM BREAK.
Arsenal: Fencing Blade & Twin Daggers
Switch weapons seamlessly at any time with Q or TAB (or keys 1 & 2):
• Fencing Blade: Strikes on quarter-note beats (1, 2, 3, 4). ±0.09s window. 10 ON / 5 OFF DMG. Max 1 attack/beat.
• Twin Daggers: Strikes on eighth-note half-beats. ±0.045s window. 4 ON / 2 OFF DMG. Max 2 attacks/beat with alternating arms.
Both weapons feed into combo, rhythm break, and 8-hit Phrase Burst (deals ×3 damage for either weapon!).
Encore Mode & Mirror Hall Arena
• Tighter Timing Windows: Fencing Blade ±0.09s ON BEAT, Twin Daggers ±0.045s ON BEAT, Perfect Parry ±0.08s.
• Encore Performance: Defeating the Conductor unlocks a 60-second free-performance Encore with 3 practice puppets, performance points, and accuracy tracking!
• Arena 3: Mirror Hall: A dark grand hall with polished side walls displaying moving reflections of the duel.
Grand Finale: The Conductor (HP 300)
Clearing Wave 3 summons The Conductor hovering above the orchestra pit with a glowing baton.
• Movement 1: ADAGIO (100 BPM): 300 → 200 HP. Swings baton every 2 bars on Beat 1 (Down / Left / Right / Thrust, 15 DMG, 3m half-plane) with a 1-bar glowing floor tell.
• Movement 2: ALLEGRO (160 BPM): 200 → 100 HP. Swings every bar alternating Down / Thrust. Bar 4 unleashes an unparriable Crescendo Sweep (20 DMG, dodge-only, spinning baton tell).
• Movement 3: CADENZA (160 BPM): 100 → 0 HP. 4-bar loop: Left → Syncopated Double Thrust (Beats 2 & 4) → Crescendo Sweep → REST (takes ×1.5 DMG!).
Elite Foe: The Rest Warden (HP 120)
A tall motionless figure wielding a tuning-fork blade (Wave 3).
• Bars 1–3 (REST): Motionless and immune (attacks deal 0 DMG).
• Bar 4 (CHARGE): Raises tuning fork with rising energy rings; still immune.
• Bar 5 Beat 1 (HEAVY STRIKE): Unleashes 20 DMG in a 2.0m arc.
• Bars 5–8 (EXPOSED): Vulnerable to all attacks until next phrase!
• Perfect Parry: Staggers Warden for 2 beats and adds +15 bonus damage to counter hits!
