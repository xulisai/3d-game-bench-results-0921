Match & Rulebook Instructions

• Set Format & Tiebreak: Win 6 games by 2 margin. At 5–5, play continues to 7–5. At 7–7, the official tiebreak decides the set: initial server serves 1 point from Deuce; thereafter servers alternate every 2 points (starting from Ad, then Deuce). Players change ends every 6 points. First to 7 by 2 margin wins (recorded as 8–7 (7–x)).

• Quick Starts: Press Enter for 0–0. Hold G + Enter for 5–5. Hold T + Enter for 7–7 Tiebreak.

• Live Stats & Replays: Press Y anytime between points to view live match statistics (Aces, Double Faults, Winners, Break Points). Aces, winners, and successful challenges trigger automated slow-motion replays (skip with Enter).

• Stamina & Hawk-Eye: Stamina degrades running speed, clean strike zones, drive depth, and first serve velocity. Press C immediately after OUT or FAULT to challenge with 3 electronic reviews.
