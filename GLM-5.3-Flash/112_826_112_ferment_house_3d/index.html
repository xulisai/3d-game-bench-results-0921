Seven vats ride the four-segment written curve — hold 20.0°, down to 12.0°, up to 18.0°, settle to 15.0° — on 20-second staggers; vat n racks at 8:00 + (n−1)×20 s. Outside the ±1.2° band, every 10 s drains −1.

The fire-wall (+1.32° per 10 s) warms all seven vats at once; the springs (−0.99° per 10 s) are per-vat. Adjacent vats more than 2° apart leak 0.1° per 10 s into each other — layout matters.

Quality grades the barrel: ≥95 S · ≥85 A · ≥70 B · else C. Each racking plays its siphon, and the barrel rack fills tier by tier — S on top. After the season, Tab opens the ledger: S×200 + A×100 + B×40 + never-out×100, rated S ≥ 950 · A ≥ 800 · B ≥ 650 · else C.

Weather seasons are written from the second brew onward — winter −2°, summer +2°, the first brew always autumn. Winter demands more wall; summer more spring. There is no curve overlay: the target lives in the readout and your memory. The strip reads every vat at a glance.
