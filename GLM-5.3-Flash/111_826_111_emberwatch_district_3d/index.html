Night Shift — Corner Grocery Fire
INCIDENT 1 OF 4 — TENEMENT
A street-front shop is burning, and the wind carries the fire through the ground floor. Take the hose, chase it room to room, and drown every flaming and smoldering cell on all three floors — the stairwell in the back-east corner climbs to the second and third storeys, and the open service shaft will let the fire breathe upward. Civilians are down at the scene — find them and carry them to the glow by the engine before their coma clock runs out. Three hydrants feed a long shift: press E beside one to hook up, and while you stay within hose reach the tank refills as you spray — step past the 25 u hose limit and the line tears free. Smoke pools room by room and chokes visibility; the thermal view reads through it. Mind the load-bearing beams: the thermal view brackets them, and 45 seconds of flame burns one through, dropping the floor around it. Every ground-floor doorway is a real door: aim the nozzle at a door to read its temperature, hose it to cool it down, and never force a hot one — it will backdraft.
