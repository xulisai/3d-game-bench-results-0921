OVERWATCH VECTOR

AIRBORNE EARLY WARNING — FIGHTER COMMAND

You fly the radar plane OVERWATCH, unarmed and untouchable, orbiting outside the battle box. Fight the war entirely by vectoring your two fighters over a deterministic radar scope. Three drones will enter the sector one after another, 40 seconds apart, on a line toward the sector center. A drone crossing within 10 units of the center is a leak — the third leak ends the patrol. Down all three and the patrol is held.

LEAD OR MISS: at launch the game solves the true lead point for the shot. Nose within 5° of it and the missile always hits — a chase and a kill flash. Beyond 5° it always misses. No probability anywhere, and no marker: the geometry lives in your head or nowhere. The first engage opens the worked tutorial.

FLIGHT OF SIX: callsigns 1 to 6, spread across three layers on fixed starting spots. Two aircraft within 3 units on the same layer raise a PROX violation and a counter — a layer difference clears it. Missiles only fire when the shooter and the target share a layer: a layer off means no shot, so sync your climbs before engaging. Press G at the title for the rally & fan-out drill — gather all six over one point, then occupy your slots with zero violations.

WAVES & LEDGERS: four training waves arrive on a fixed schedule — T+30 / 60 / 90 / 120, one or two drones each, rotating through the corner gates, straight lines to the sector center at 8 u/s. Fuel burns 4.4 per 10 s of cruise (52.8 burned by T+120); below 25 the strip runs red. Missiles stay 4 per fighter. A drone crossing within 10 units of the center undestroyed is a leak — the banner now reads −100, and the score ledger settles on the wave board when all four waves are cleared.

GHOST IMAGES: from wave five on, each wave opens with a 75-second jamming window — the wave's blips render as paired ghosts: true image and false image 3 units apart, the false offset a fixed +90° from the true heading. Engage a ghost and the missile chases nothing — ammunition wasted; the offset can be learned and reversed. A radar-edge shimmer marks the active window. Press J at the title for the ghost drill: kill one drone inside a window.

THE RING: a four-engine HVT transport crosses the box from west to east on layer 2 at 3 u/s from T+150, carrying 3 hull points. Its 20-unit escort ring renders live with the count of friendly fighters inside: with 2 or more inside, enemy attacks are deterred entirely; with fewer than 2, any enemy lingering 20 units from the HVT for 5 seconds strikes it once (−1 HP). 0 HP is a mission-fail line. Seeing the transport safely out the east side logs a survival credit of +100 on the score ledger.

THE TANKER: a wide-body tanker with a trailing boom orbits (50, 50) on layer 2. Press K to send a selected fighter to it — but only while its fuel reads below 40. On arrival within 5 units the fighter plugs in: +25 fuel per 10 s of station time, automatic departure at 90. The boom serves one fighter at a time — a second fighter ordered in holds a waiting orbit 5–8 units off and plugs in the moment the boom frees, in the order the orders were given; the queue is visible on the scope. While plugged in the fighter refuses ENGAGE — refueling is an occupation. Over a 420-second campaign each fighter burns 184.8 fuel, so at least one tanker visit per fighter is arithmetic, not preference — deciding who goes and when is the whole problem.

THE FULL WAR (G): ten fixed waves, fourteen drones in all, from T+30 to T+300 — and the waves come in on different layers, so every engagement starts with a synced climb or descent. Wave 5 runs jamming. Waves 6 and 9 are FEINTS: they turn away 20 seconds after entry and leave the box — they never reach the center line and never count as leaks. Shooting at a feint wastes missiles and positions; reading it and withholding is the correct play, reproducible every time. The campaign settles when the tenth wave resolves: kills, leaks, HVT state. After the war, Tab opens the Battle Ledger — kills × 100, HVT survived +400, zero-leak bonus +200 with the transport home, leaks × −100 — rated S ≥ 1300 / A ≥ 1000 / B ≥ 750 / C. A drone downed while feinting still counts as a kill: the ledger pays for fallen metal, not judgment.

DETERMINISTIC INTERCEPT GEOMETRY

Target at (0, 0) flying +x at 8.8 u/s. Your fighter at (0, 10) doing 10 u/s.
In time t the shot must cover the growing gap: 10t = √(77.44t² + 100) → 100t² = 77.44t² + 100 → t ≈ 2.10 s.
Lead point = target + velocity × t = (8.8 × 2.10, 0) ≈ (18.5, 0).
Fire with your nose within 5° of the bearing to that point: inside 5° the missile always hits, beyond 5° it always misses. The solution is geometry — solve it in your head from the target's track and your position, then bring your fighter inside 15 units on the right bearing. Practice all three geometries: title, then P.
