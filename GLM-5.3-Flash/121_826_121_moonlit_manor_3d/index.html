MOONLIT MANOR

A gentleman-thief's legend night — three manors, one run, one score.
Tonight the coach carries you to three manors: HOLLOWBROOK HOUSE, the MARLOWE GALLERY, and the VALE OBSERVATORY. In each one, slip past the patrol, crack the wall safe in the F3 study (0, 5), carry its prize out through the east garden gate — and the coach rolls on to the next house. Get caught once and the night is over: the manors left unvisited are lost. Guards see 60° ahead at 14 m (7 m against a crouched thief in shadow) and hear your footsteps — running carries far, walking near, a crouch is nearly silent. Three stones per manor: silent to throw, loud on landing. Sweeping cameras trip the alarm after 1.5 s in their gaze; flip a breaker box to black them out for 60 s. Eight treasures per manor — grab three at a time with E and bank them at your loot bag. The night ledger (P) tallies manors ×250, the ghost bonus ×300 and treasures ×40.
