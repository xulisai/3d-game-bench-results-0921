Shift job: land 3 containers on the feeder ship within 90 seconds. Every start lags and every stop overshoots — sway past 25° sounds the buzzer, past 35° the swing itself is an impact. Loads up to 40 t latch silently; heavier latches hot; at 45 t the hook refuses. The 60 t unit on the heavy pad stays where it is.
Placements are scored by offset from the slot or bay center at the moment of release: within 0.25 u is PERFECT (+100), within 0.6 u is GOOD (+60), wider is OUT OF CELL — the box sits askew across the slot edge until you lift it and re-place it (the re-placement replaces the attempt). Released above an empty tier, a box simply falls until it finds support and scores wherever it rests. Work the rehandle drill strip.
The feeder takes a fixed stowage: bays A–D and the midship cradle, three tiers each, masses as printed on the plan panel — equal masses are interchangeable, wrong masses fill the cell without credit. The balance ledger reads θ = Σ(m·x)/120 over everything resting on the ship; it runs red past 2° and a perfect plan nets STOWAGE BALANCED. The cradle sits at lever arm zero.
The berth clock also drives the wind schedule — calm to 70 s, L1 LIGHT until 160 s (loads lean −x), then L2 STRONG until 280 s (lean +x), then L3 GUST — a ±4° square wave, 12 s period — until 340 s, and calm to the end. A hanging load settles about 0.35 u off-lever at light and 0.70 u at strong — park the trolley upwind by that much to drop plumb. From L2 up a wind whistle rides the audio; wind pushes with or without a load and touches nothing else.
Five trucks deliver the rest of the plan on a fixed clock — T1 at 30 s (30 t), T2 at 90 s (20 t), T3 at 150 s (40 t), T4 at 230 s (30 t), T5 at 300 s (20 t). Each enters the portal lane at x+16 and stops at x+10; if the dock is taken the truck holds at x+14.5 and advances when the bay clears — trucks wait without limit. Latch a bedded box at its lock point 2.4; the moment a truck stops, the job panel lists the open cells that match its box. A truck departs west once its bed stays clear.
The twin-hook tandem rig — press F to arm — latches both hooks at once when the trolley is centered over the 60 t unit's paired lift lugs (both within 0.4 u) as you lower onto them: 30 t per hook. While attached, every axis runs at half speed and half acceleration; carry the unit to the midship cradle — a normal alignment target whose lever arm is zero, so the tilt readout holds — and SPACE with support releases both hooks and restores full speeds. An overweight 44 t export box stands on the inspection pad: single-hook it onto the weighbridge to close the job — the latch holds under an OVERLOAD WARNING, while the 60 t unit still refuses a single hook outright.
Night shift. The yard runs dark — towers and girder floodlights pool warm light, and the trolley's steerable searchlight (U/O pans it ±45° from straight down, west/east) is your reading lamp: slot corner brackets, ship cell frames, the truck-stop marker and pad outlines render only inside its beam — pan away and they vanish, pan back and they return. Above 1 u/s the gantry flashes its travel lamps and sounds a low horn.
The hoist hydraulics run weak on two fixed clock windows — 100–140 s and 250–290 s: a HYDRAULIC LOW banner shows and the hoist rate drops from 1.5 to 1.0 u/s — tandem halves whatever is current (0.75 outside, 0.5 inside). Gantry and trolley are untouched, and wind, trucks, sway and the bands keep their own clocks — the late window overlaps L2 wind and the 300 s truck, each arriving exactly on schedule.
The whole shift binds into one Shift Score — press TAB (empty hook) to audit it any time; at clock 360 the berth settles and the screen opens on its own: plan cells ×100 (twelve counting the cradle — wrong masses and out-of-cell landings never count), alignment average ×3 (PERFECT 100 / GOOD 60 by final placement), +200 when all twelve are correct and |θ| ≤ 2°, and −40 per damage event. Ratings: S ≥ 900 · A ≥ 750 · B ≥ 600 · C below — a perfect zero-damage shift tops out at 1700. R on the score screen is the master reset.
