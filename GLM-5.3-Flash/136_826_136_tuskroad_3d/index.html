TUSKROAD

A Jungle Freight Haul

Ride Tee down the jungle post road to the sawmill stacking yard at (120, −40). Six teak logs (0.4 t each) and a timber crib of 6 bays wait there. Stack all six log-cabin style — three along the bottom bays, three crosswise on top.

A released log falls straight down from the trunk — line the axis up within ±10°, land it inside the bay marks, and raise the load (R) to clear the crib for the top layer.

The post road runs on east past the sawmill to the far depot. Twelve dead trees stand along it, each with 3 hit points and a fixed fall. Ram one at 5 u/s or faster — three solid hits and it topples where it must: seven span the gullies as log bridges, five flatten the briar patches into road. Slower bumps do nothing. Press M for the map of every detour versus its cut.

Tee's back rack carries two bays of 0.5 t each — aim the crosshair at a bay and press E to stash the trunk load or pull it back out. Every bay in the ledger slows the walk (×0.9, then ×0.75), stacked with the trunk-carry ×0.85. Crates (0.3 t) wait by the camp and the yard and ride the rack like logs; overweight bays are refused.

A deep river bars the way east: wade the marked ford (depth 1.4 — ×0.7, splashing); the dark channel refuses entry at its edge. Mud flats (×0.6) lie north of the post road and beside the east reach — every factor stacks in the ledger.

Four bee hives hang beside the road — come within 3 units and the swarm chases for 5 s at half speed (cargo stays put). Press F to fire the trunk jet — 10 units of reach, 3 charges in the tank; a hit scatters a swarm for 8 s. Refill at any water trough — the camp, the sawmill, and the hill station — at 2 s per charge.

Press Tab for the post run: three stages bind the route — camp → sawmill (haul two logs), sawmill → hill station (220,60) (fell two shortcut trees, haul two crates across the mud), hill station → river terminus (300,−20) (ford the crossing, skirt the hives). Stages unlock in order; three side jobs pay 100 each. Outside a stage the road is free-roam.

Watch the sky: at 07:00 on the clock rain falls, and at 08:30 the river rises a full unit — the ford goes from 1.4 to 2.4 deep and refuses entry until the water falls back. Stage 3's ford must be beaten before the rise; the HUD shows your safe margin. Hold T to wait out the early hours — it runs hotter before the storm.

When the run is bound, Tab opens the season score — stages ×200, side jobs ×100, trees felled ×50, accidents −60 (body knocks, crushes, spilled cargo). S from 900.
