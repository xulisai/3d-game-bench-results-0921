ECHO EDGE

The ruin holds three battlefields. Everything you do is recorded into the ring for twelve seconds; the golden echo that follows replays it frame by frame, at its recorded world positions — it does not follow you across rooms. Clear the forecourt garrison, advance through the Echo Gallery, and end the expedition on the Twin Moon Terraces. Land your swing and your echo's swing on the same foe within half a second to loose a Resonance Slash; bank three and press F for the echo's Whirlwind Coda. Wraiths shrug off steel; archers loose at where you or your echo stood; and no blade reaches across a ledge taller than a fighter — every swing obeys the height between you. On the terraces the Shadowbinders come: robed hunters who ignore you utterly, chase your echo, and chain it in place — break the bind with your own steel, or the echo's frames are lost. And when the terraces fall quiet, the Mirror Blade Warden walks again: he lunges at whichever of you struck last, flanked by two elite binders of his own.
