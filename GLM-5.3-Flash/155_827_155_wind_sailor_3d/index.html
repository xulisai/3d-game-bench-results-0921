Wind Sailor

A triangle race against the fleet — three marks, three AI skippers, one clean lap.

←→ or AD — rudder (steer)
↑↓ or WS — trim the sail
Enter start signal  ·  R restart race

Sail the course in order: beat upwind to Mark 1 (leave to port), run downwind to Mark 2 (leave to starboard), reach across to Mark 3 (leave to starboard), then cross the start/finish line. A mark only counts if you pass it on the required side — the ring around the next mark shows the side and the way round.

Race the fleet. Three AI skippers sail the same wind and the same physics: they tack upwind, hunt the favored side as the wind veers and backs, and round the marks — but they misjudge laylines and trim now and then, so clean sailing beats them. Sail directly downwind of another boat and you slide into its wind shadow and slow. On a crossing, starboard tack has right of way: a port-tack boat must keep clear. Collisions cost every boat involved +15 s. Finishing ahead of the fleet lifts your rating: each place behind the leader adds a 20 s allowance to your effective time.
Ratings: Gold ≤ 4:00 · Silver ≤ 5:10 · Bronze ≤ 6:30 (fleet-adjusted).
