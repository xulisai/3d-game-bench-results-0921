The envelope lifts off once it passes 65 °C — and it overshoots: every degree you bank keeps carrying you higher after the burner stops. Six wind layers stack from the meadow to the 120 m ceiling; the vane reads only your own layer, so climbing into a new band swings your drift. Touch down on the X at E 180, inside the 30 m ring — a gentle arrival is 2 m/s or less. Press A to drop marker bags at the gold ring at E 140: the bag drifts with your layer's wind, so lead the target. Every launch loads 100.0 units of propane — LOW burns it frugally, HIGH fast — and at zero the burner clicks dead for good. Hold T to fast-forward. On this card, press 1 to race the judge's checkered hare and drop your two markers at its X, or 2 for the judge's three declared goals — one pre-assigned bag per goal, served in order, or 3 for the FLY-IN: choose one of three written launch pads, carry two bags to the painted goal F at (120, 360), and your best bag score adds to the printed site bonus. The morning winds are filed, and they move: at T+ 240 s the L1 east wind turns north, at T+ 420 s the L2 east wind turns north too — press M in flight for the morning briefing exactly as filed at dawn; the vane always reads the live truth. The eastern ridge, its power corridor and the bird lake are hard terrain — the visible seams are exactly the rules.
