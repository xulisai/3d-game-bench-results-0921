ROADWEAVE

A tiny commuting city — connect the colors, the cars do the rest.

  • DRAG  lay chalk road tile by tile — drag over road to erase (refunds)
  • C  swap 45° / top-down camera
  • ENTER  found the city / raise the next dawn
  • R  master reset to this title card
  • TAB  city ledger — score & rating after the run

Seven dawns raise twenty-two houses from a written schedule — six red, six blue, five green, five yellow; the same day always builds the same houses. Any two same-color houses pair for commuting, chained 1–2, 2–3, … — every pair under its own 12 s / four-car caps. The tile budget starts at 40 and grows +5 at 20 / 50 / 100 total deliveries. Close each 90-second day with 3 deliveries for a DAY N OPEN card. If three or more houses stand red-faced at once, the city falls: the day ends, its deliveries are voided — the next dawn clears the faces and the run continues. From day three, two written rivers cross the city — water is impassable until you bridge it: a bridge span costs 2 tiles, and spans already laid when the river arrives become bridges free. A straight run of 6+ road tiles becomes a highway: double speed, and no turning — enter and leave on ordinary tiles. Survive to the seventh sunset for the grand settlement: total deliveries, days survived, and the red-face history. TAB then opens the city ledger — score = deliveries × 10 + days survived × 100 − red faces × 30, rated S ≥ 900 / A ≥ 750 / B ≥ 600 / C. Cars drive the shortest path, keep a 1.35 u gap, stop for the synced 3.5 s traffic lights, and re-plan at the next tile whenever you tear road up.
