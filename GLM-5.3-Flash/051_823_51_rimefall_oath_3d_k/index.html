Wolf Stance: slashes of 6 / 8 / 11 chain into a flurry — press J again within 0.5 seconds to continue; K lunges 4 units for 14. Bear Stance: slower but brutal — cleaves of 13 / 17 and a 22 overhead smash through the Rime Shieldbearer's guard. Sidestep through the first 0.2 seconds of an attack to gain a Rime charge. L spends one to hurl a Rimeshard: 10 damage that freezes lesser enemies solid. A Raider Skald watches the ship camp and another the gate — their spears strike where you stood, so step aside after the throw. The lake ice is slick: everything slides, and snow squalls blind the pass every 20 seconds. Break a foe's posture and fear scatters its packmates; slay a wolf and its kin burn with red-eyed fury. Two camps hold the pass — by the longship and before the gate — and each falls together. Clear all seven. In the far northwestern field sleeps Jarl Rimebark: mirror his stance — only the opposing style breaks his guard — and mind his wrath. At 175 his wrath breaks the lake: three fissures of black water open and a Frost Tomb joins his rotation. At 20 he falls — finish him with J at arm's reach. And should you fall, the bonfire calls you back. The gate still holds while his wards stand: cull all seven to break the seal — death re-seals it. The slain Jarl drops the Heart of the Rimebark: step onto it to claim an Oathmark and read the hunt report. Hold Tab for the report at any time.
