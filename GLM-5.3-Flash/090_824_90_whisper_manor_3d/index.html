WHISPER MANOR

a candlelit victorian haunting
The manor is dark, and the Shade walks its rooms. Gather the seven diary pages — five below and two in the bedrooms upstairs, up the stair beside the foyer's west wall — then open the vault behind the foyer's north wall and crack the great safe that waits inside: its brass plaque names the pages that whisper the combination, and every wrong turn groans loudly enough for the dead to hear. Your candle wax drains with every second — faster while you sneak — relight it at the candelabras or at the wax caches hidden up in the attic. Broken glass litters the halls (step over it carefully), doors swing shut with E, a brass Clockwork Sentry marches both floors on its rounds (deaf, but its eyes are keen), and a pale Wick Wraith drifts the ground floor: within two paces its chill snuffs twenty wax from your candle, wherever you stand. And when your candle dies, darkness emboldens the Shade: it roams room to room at a hunter's pace until wax rises above zero and light restores the old rules. A hidden panel in the study shows itself to a candle still half full. Opening the great safe begins the Ritual of Five Flames: light all five candelabras within ninety seconds, for each flame gutters out sixty seconds after it is lit. Succeed, and the manor answers — and your reckoning is tallied.
