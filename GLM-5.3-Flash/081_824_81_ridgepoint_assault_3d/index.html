C9 MODEL INPUT — ONE CONTINUOUS CAMPAIGN

Strongpoint ALPHA is held by E1–E3; strongpoint BRAVO to the east by E4 and E5. You lead a two-man fireteam: VEGA and ROOK follow your formation, engage hostile targets on their own, and concentrate fire on whatever you aim at. If a squadmate falls, reach them within 10 seconds and hold E beside them to revive. Capture ALPHA to unlock BRAVO — your spawn advances with the line. Ammo crates with orange flags refill your rifle. Bullets drop with range. Watch for tracer fire: sustained fire from multiple defenders SUPPRESSES your aim, and their flankers will swing wide around your advance. You carry two smoke grenades (G — blocks sight for six seconds) and two frags (T — 3 m kill radius; mind your feet). Behind BRAVO a ramp climbs to the ridge-top strongpoint CHARLIE; headcount decides contested captures, so keep your fireteam close. Taking CHARLIE is not the end — hold the ridge against four waves of counterattackers or the mission fails. Captured defenders fall back to the next point, crates resupply once each, and the final score rewards a fast, clean campaign.
