GRIFFON POST

Carry two mail bags down the valley — Lakeside Halt first, then Canyon Keep. Each station has a receiving window: deliver inside it for full pay, arrive late for half, arrive too early and the station refuses the mail. Canyon Keep's mail is FRAGILE: hold G above 2.0 for 0.4 s or crash, and it is destroyed.
