SECTOR I — THE ICEFIELD CORRIDOR

POLARIS TREK

Trek north up the corridor to the survey flag, then push on for the pole beacon itself. The cold drains your body temperature — the campfire at base camp is your only warmth, and it will not burn forever. Two supply cabins stand along the route — Cache Alpha on the plateau, Cache Bravo on the open ice. Shelter inside, feed their stoves with fuel cans, eat what you find, and collect both map fragments. Cross the plateau crevasse field on its snow bridges: the dark cracks are bottomless. An old sled waits at the top of the descent — mount it and ride the berm-lined track down to the ice. Past the open ice a serac labyrinth bars the route — thread its gaps. Collected map fragments assemble the expedition map — press M to read it. Blizzards roll through on a fixed schedule — heed the warnings — and once each expedition a whiteout swallows the route whole; only the stake lamps, the aurora, and the station beacon pierce it. At dusk the light fails into polar night, and the green aurora above the Pole Station becomes your truest bearing. The final ascent is deep snow — pace yourself, ride out the summit storm, and reach the Pole Station door to complete the expedition.
